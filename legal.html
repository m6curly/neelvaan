Acceptable use

Local processing

This release has no NEELVAAN account sign-in or cloud workspace storage. Selected raster files are processed in your browser. Named Place Mark layers, their place names, descriptions, icons and coordinates are saved in this browser's local storage for the same website origin so they remain available after a reload. They are not uploaded or synchronised to a server, another browser or another device. You can remove a layer in Map Content or clear this site's browser storage. Keep your own copies of source files and exports.

03 · THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL

Data sources, copyright & attribution

NEELVAAN is a platform interface, not the owner of every dataset or basemap displayed or processed in it. The correct attribution and licence must travel with the relevant map, dataset, report or export. A single “© NEELVAAN” notice does not replace third-party attribution.

OpenStreetMap data

OpenStreetMap data is © OpenStreetMap contributors and is made available under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0. The OSM History tool requests object history from the OpenStreetMap API. Attribution should be shown as © OpenStreetMap contributors, linked to the OSM copyright page, with the ODbL licence linked where data is distributed.

ODbL share-alike duties may apply to a publicly used derivative database or substantial extraction/re-utilisation; whether a particular dataset or Produced Work qualifies depends on the nature of the use. Review the ODbL legal text rather than assuming every map image and every dataset has identical obligations.

OpenStreetMap copyright & licence · OSMF attribution guidelines · ODbL 1.0 legal text

OpenStreetMap standard map tiles

Interactive map tiles from tile.openstreetmap.org are a separate service from the underlying OSM open data. Visible map attribution is required. The public tile service is best-effort and does not permit bulk tile scraping, prefetching or offline tile packages.

OSM Standard tile usage policy · OSM API usage policy

Other basemap and imagery providers

The OSM History workspace can display style layers from HOT, OSM France, OpenStreetMap.de, Lima Labs, CyclOSM, OpenTopoMap-R, OpenHikingMap, Esri and NASA GIBS. The raster workspaces may use OpenStreetMap tiles or Esri World Imagery. The map's own attribution control is the primary source credit for the selected layer. Each provider has its own licence, attribution wording, fair-use rules, geographic coverage and commercial-use restrictions; these can change independently of NEELVAAN.

Esri services and data are not automatically public domain. Esri's web-site/service terms distinguish non-commercial and commercial use, and Esri India has additional local terms for certain datasets/services. Obtain the appropriate licence before embedding Esri content in a revenue-generating service or redistributing it. Do not remove the provider's attribution.

Esri web site and service terms · Esri India legal information · NASA GIBS

Landsat / USGS data

The LST workspace is designed for Landsat 8/9 Collection 2 Level-2 ST_B10 data and associated QA bands. USGS states that Landsat data are in the public domain and requests acknowledgement of USGS as the source. A recommended dataset citation is: Earth Resources Observation and Science (EROS) Center. (2020). Landsat 8-9 Operational Land Imager / Thermal Infrared Sensor Level-2, Collection 2 [dataset]. U.S. Geological Survey. Use the product-specific citation supplied by USGS for the actual scene and version. A collection-level citation is available at DOI: 10.5066/P9OGBGM6.

USGS Landsat Collection 2 Level-2 products · USGS public-domain statement

User-provided raster data

Change Detection accepts user-supplied, comparable GeoTIFF rasters. NEELVAAN does not automatically know their origin, licence, sensor, units, processing history or publication restrictions. You are responsible for checking those rights and including the source citation, licence and acknowledgements required by the data provider in any report or map you publish.

External software and libraries

The Platform loads third-party browser libraries and fonts, including Leaflet, proj4, geotiff.js and Google Fonts. Their respective authors, maintainers and providers retain their rights and licence terms. These libraries are used as dependencies and are not owned by NEELVAAN.

04 · ACCURACY & RELIANCE

Geospatial analysis disclaimer

Maps, geocoding results, OSM history, raster comparisons and land-surface-temperature calculations are provided for exploration and analytical assistance. They may be incomplete, delayed, misclassified, spatially inaccurate or affected by upstream outages, sensor limitations, raster alignment, NoData handling, cloud contamination, projection, resolution and browser/device constraints.

  • OSM data is community-contributed and may not represent current ground conditions or official boundaries.
  • Change Detection compares pixel values in two rasters after grid checks. It does not perform image registration, atmospheric correction, radiometric normalisation, cross-sensor harmonisation or automatic cloud masking.
  • LST results represent satellite-derived land surface temperature, not near-surface air temperature. Product quality limits and missing retrievals can affect results.
  • Area measurements, geometry reconstructions and visual overlays are estimates based on available source data and implemented assumptions. They are not surveys, title records or legal boundary determinations.
  • Always validate consequential findings using authoritative datasets, field verification and qualified professional review.

To the extent permitted by applicable law, the Platform is provided “as is” and “as available”, without a promise that outputs are fit for a particular purpose. Nothing in these terms excludes liability that cannot lawfully be excluded.
